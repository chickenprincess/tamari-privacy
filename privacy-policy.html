Privacy Policy

Tamari  ·  Last updated: October 27, 2026

Tamari ("we", "our", or "us") is a Georgian language learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide and improve the app:

  • Account information – your name and email address, provided when you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • User ID – an internal identifier assigned to your account by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Learning progress – which lessons, flashcards, and exercises you have completed, and your quiz scores. This is stored so your progress persists across devices and sessions.
  • Purchase history – records of any in-app purchases (such as a premium subscription), used solely to unlock the features you paid for.

We do not collect location data, health data, browsing history, contacts, photos, or any sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • App functionality – to authenticate you, save your progress, and enable features you have purchased.
  • Transactional email – to send account confirmation and password-reset emails. We do not send marketing email without your explicit opt-in.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Tamari uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase – authentication and database hosting. Your account data and progress are stored on Supabase servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In – if you choose to sign in with Google, Google's OAuth service is used. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchases – purchases are processed by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend – used to deliver transactional emails (confirmation links, password resets). See resend.com/privacy.
  • Google Gemini API – used on-device to generate audio pronunciations of Georgian text. Text sent to this API is not stored or linked to your identity.

4. Data Linked to You

The following data types are linked to your identity in Tamari:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Purchase history
  • Product interaction (learning progress)

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7). When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Tamari is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to how we process it. To exercise any of these rights, or to delete your account, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Authentication is handled by Supabase, which uses industry-standard security practices. We do not store passwords in plain text.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
